# NOTICE

The markup documents of this crate are converted from the Fluent theme of the
upstream project (Avalonia, MIT; see the `NOTICE.md` at the root of the
repository). The conversion changes names only (`scripts/convert_theme_xaml.py`).
Some of the upstream documents derive from third-party sources and carry the
notice of their origin as their header comment, which is kept verbatim.
