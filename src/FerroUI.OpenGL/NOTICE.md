# NOTICE

The contracts and the entry point tables of this crate are ported from the
`Avalonia.OpenGL` project of Avalonia (MIT; see the `NOTICE.md` at the root
of the repository).

`gl_consts.rs` is generated by `scripts/convert-gl-consts.py` from the
constant table of that project, which is a transcription of the OpenGL
headers of the Khronos Group (constant names and values).
