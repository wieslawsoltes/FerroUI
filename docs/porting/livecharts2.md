# LiveCharts2 port

FerroUI ports [LiveCharts2](https://github.com/Live-Charts/LiveCharts2) (MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 Alberto Rodriguez Orozco) at commit `8537b90f` as its advanced chart library: Cartesian, pie, polar, Sankey, treemap and geographic charts with LiveCharts2's measurement, paint and motion. `ferroui-controls-charts` (#282) stays the simple chart library (line, area and bar series, axes, legends). The work comes from pull request #312, which stays a draft reference and is closed when the last part lands (owner decisions, 2026-10-30; the review is in the pull request record).

## Accounting

`scripts/livecharts2/inventory.py` reads a LiveCharts2 checkout at the pin of `docs/porting/livecharts2/sources.lock.json` (the Git objects of that commit, never the working tree) and FerroUI's Roslyn syntax extractor (`scripts/api-extract`, .NET 10), and accounts for every source file, API member and test declaration of the pin: 698 source files, 4,859 API members and 1,066 test declarations.

```sh
python3 scripts/livecharts2/inventory.py --upstream ../LiveCharts2
python3 scripts/livecharts2/inventory.py --check --upstream ../LiveCharts2
```

Committed in `docs/porting/livecharts2/` (O8 of `winui-controls.md`, the pattern of the WinUI Gallery scanner): the pin, the reviewed `mapping.json`, and the generated `summary.json` and `REPORT.md`. The inventories and maps (`inventory.json`, `upstream-api.json`, `source-map.json`, `api-map.json`, `test-map.json`) are written to `target/livecharts2-scan`, an artifact of the workflow.

Every entry is `pending` until `mapping.json` records a reviewed disposition: `ported` or `native-replacement` with the Rust files that implement it (which must exist), `partial` with what remains, or `not-applicable` with the reason. Each part of the port adds the dispositions it implements. The counts are declarations, not executed tests or behavioural equivalence.

The `scan` job of `livecharts.yml` checks out the pin, runs `inventory.py --check`, puts the report in the run summary and keeps the inventories as an artifact. It runs on the paths of the port and nightly.

### What the snapshot review found

Reviewing the native renders beside the upstream references of the 55 scenarios of L3 found differences, each named in the disposition of its scenario. #442 closes these, each against the pinned source and with a test in `cartesian_chart_engine.rs`:

- Box series: the visible box series of a chart stand side by side in each category (`SeriesContext.GetBoxPosition`, `BoxMeasureHelper`, with the source's padding 5 and the theme's maximum width 60) and are drawn as `BoxGeometry`: whiskers from the box to the extremes, the median across, no caps, the theme's dark outline.
- Axis ranges: an axis without data spans ten unit widths from zero and an axis of zero width grows by half a unit width on each side (`CartesianChartEngine.Measure`) before its labels, ticks and padding are measured; a series requests its padding from the tick of its raw bounds (`Extensions.GetTick`), and the separators take their step from the draw margin (`CoreAxis.BeginMeasure`). A single remaining point now spans 0.5 to 1.5 with eleven separators, as the upstream image.
- Stacked step areas: the lower edge of an area is the upper edge of the series below walked backwards, so no cell between the series is left unfilled.
- Theme defaults: the MaterialDesign500 palette; the area at alpha 50 under line, step, range and polar lines (a transparent fill is the source's `Fill = null`); stacked areas in full colour without a stroke or geometry; line series bound their points, not the pivot; axis labels of 16 px with the theme's Cartesian padding of 12 (`LabelsPadding` is optional: `None` takes 12 for a Cartesian axis and the source's 3 for a polar axis).

One difference is the upstream's: for an empty series (`LineSeriesTests.VectorOperationsClear`) the reference image draws the X axis from 10 to 0, because the source's `Scaler` passes the sentinel bounds of the series to `AxisLimit.ValidateLimits` in swapped order. The native axis keeps the default range from 0 to 10 in the order of the axis; the disposition of the scenario says so.

Still different from the source theme (named in the disposition of every scenario): the gauge rules (no stroke, centred data labels, corner radius 8, the translucent fill series; the gauge scenarios set them), the stroke width and the light 12 px markers of line series, the corner radius and the missing stroke of bars, the scatter and candle colours, and the separators of X axes, which the theme hides.

### L4: the polar chart

`polar_chart_engine.rs` of `ferroui-livecharts` starts from the code of #312, checked against the pinned `PolarChartEngine`, `CorePolarAxis` and `CorePolarLineSeries`: independent angle and radius axes, the empty and zero-width bounds of the source, the series padding in polar ticks, the automatic margin of the angle labels, an explicit draw margin, fit-to-bounds, the source spline of an open or closed line with null splitting, stacked radii, the five data label positions with the tangent and cotangent rotation flags, and hover areas. A polar line fills the theme's area over the same path it strokes; an open line closes its area with a straight edge, as the source fills its open path. The angular inverse uses atan2 and corrects the quadrant and nonzero-minimum defects of the source.

`ferroui-controls-livecharts` adds `ChartKind::Polar` (the enum stays `non_exhaustive` for the Sankey, treemap and geographic charts), the generated `PolarChart` (its X axes are the angle axes and its Y axes the radius axes; `InitialRotation` -90 from `LiveChartsSettings`) and `PolarLineSeries`, `Chart::polar_scale`, and angle and radius conversions in `scale_data_to_pixels` and `scale_pixels_to_data`. The scene is drawn by the same native renderer as the Cartesian and pie scenes, so it takes the GPU path of every backend. The ControlCatalog has the page PolarChart, and `snapshots.rs` restates the five scenarios of the upstream `PolarChartTests` (60 scenarios in all).

`mapping.json` records `PolarLineSeriesTest.ShouldScaleProperly` and `PolarDrawMarginTests.ExplicitDrawMargin_IsHonored` as ported, the five polar snapshot scenarios and the three polar source files as partial: the report counts 96 ported and 71 partial test declarations. `PolarLineSeriesTest.ShouldPlaceToolTipsCorrectly` stays pending with the tooltip suites.

### L4b: the Sankey and treemap charts

`treemap_chart_engine.rs` and `sankey_chart_engine.rs` of `ferroui-livecharts` start from the code of #312, checked line by line against the pinned `TreemapLayout`, `TreemapChartEngine`, `CoreTreemapSeries`, `SankeyLayout`, `SankeyChartEngine`, `CoreSankeySeries` and the SkiaSharp Sankey geometries. The treemap engine has the squarified layout, the roll-up of internal nodes (a nonzero explicit value wins; an empty child list is an internal node, without a hover area or a label), the padding inset of every level, the partition of the draw margin between the visible series by their totals (a series without a slot takes the whole margin), hover areas and centred labels on the leaves only, a label culled when its text with the label padding does not fit its tile, and the paint order of the source: the fills of a series, then its strokes, then the labels of every series. The Sankey engine has the d3-sankey vertical layout (repeated nodes, self-loops, unknown ends and invalid weights skipped, Kahn depths with the cycle error, the column scale, the relaxation sweeps and collision passes, the link bands in the order of their other ends), the bipartite arc layout with its error on multi-column data, the ribbon, chord and arc segment shapes, the Auto, Outside and Inside labels with the room they reserve and the radial label margin of the arcs, the ribbon paint (the link colour, else the source node colour at half alpha, else `LinkFill`, else the theme's at alpha 90; a null `LinkFill` takes the node fill) and the paint order of the source (ribbons, node strokes, node fills, labels). The code of #312 had five more label positions and per-node treemap colours, which the source does not; they are gone.

Differences from the source, named in the dispositions: nodes and links are objects (the engine identifies them by id), and a node or link colour stands for `NodeColorMapper` and `LinkColorMapper`; the treemap model is the native `TreemapNode`, with no `ValueMapper`, `ChildrenMapper` or `LabelMapper` for other models; `DataLabelsMaxWidth` does not wrap labels; a cycle, a bipartite arc of three columns and a treemap tree that contains itself are a `ChartError` where the source throws or recurses without end; the squarified weights are normalised by the largest, so weights near `f64::MAX` keep their areas; positions are `f64` where the source uses `float`. The default theme of the source gives a treemap no fill, which draws nothing; the native treemap takes the palette colour, as the theme does for a Sankey series.

`ferroui-controls-livecharts` adds `ChartKind::Sankey` and `ChartKind::Treemap` (`ChartKind` stays `non_exhaustive` for the geographic chart), the generated `SankeyChart`, `TreemapChart`, `SankeySeries` (the properties of `ISankeySeries` with the source defaults) and `TreemapSeries` (the shared `Padding`, 2 by default, and `CornerRadius`), the collections `SankeyNodes`, `SankeyLinks` and `TreemapNodes`, and the models `SankeyNode`, `SankeyLink` and `TreemapNode` (`node_models.rs`), observed down to every child list. A link refers to its node objects, so markup declares a treemap completely while a Sankey graph is built in code; the catalog page SankeyChart builds its two graphs in its class. The ControlCatalog has the pages SankeyChart and TreemapChart, and `snapshots.rs` restates the nine image scenarios of `SankeySeriesTests` and the five of `TreemapSeriesTests` (74 scenarios in all); the hover scenarios move the pointer, so their renders show the native tooltip.

`mapping.json` records as ported `SankeySeriesTests.BipartiteArc_ThrowsOnMultiColumn`, the four Sankey cases of `SeriesHitTests`, the Sankey and treemap cases of `CollapsedDrawMarginAllChartsTests` and the four Sankey and treemap cases of `_MemoryTests`; the fourteen image scenarios as partial, each with the engine test of what it pins; and 23 source files: the two layouts and the three node models ported, the two engines and the two core series partial, and the measure contexts, the base and SkiaSharp geometries, the SkiaSharp series and the interfaces native replacements. The report counts 107 ported and 85 partial test declarations. `SharedUITests.SankeyChartTests.ShouldLoad` and `TreemapChartTests.ShouldLoad` stay pending with the UI suites.

### L4c: the geographic map

`geo/` of `ferroui-livecharts` ports `LiveChartsCore/Geo` at the pin, checked against the code of #312: the default (equirectangular), Mercator and orthographic projectors with their default bounds (the whole earth; -65 to 85 degrees for Mercator), the normalized bounds of the source (`NaN` or `None` to the default, an inverted pair swapped, an equal pair to the defaults), the preferred ratios, the projections and their inverses (the orthographic one rejects a position outside the disc), `Maps.BuildProjector`, which fits the preferred ratio into the map area centred on the shorter side, and maps read from GeoJSON into named layers of lands (`DrawnMap`, `MapLayer`, `LandDefinition`, `LandData`): `Polygon` and `MultiPolygon` features, the lower-cased `name`, `shortName` and `setOf` properties with their case-insensitive fallback, `feature_<index>` for a feature without a name, every ring a land shape sorted by the diagonal of its bounds, and the `<short name>_<index>` key of a short name read twice. `heat_functions.rs` ports `HeatFunctions`.

The world map is the source's `src/LiveChartsCore/Geo/world.geojson`, copied byte for byte (178,383 bytes, 177 countries keyed by their ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 codes in lower case) and embedded with `include_str!` as the source embeds it as a resource; `Maps::world_map` parses it once. It is distributed with LiveCharts2 under its MIT license, which names no other license or origin for it; its features match the public-domain 1:110m Admin 0 countries of Natural Earth. The attribution is in the `NOTICE.md` of the crate. The JSON compresses to about 55 KB in the `.crate` package of `ferroui-livecharts`, well under the size limit of crates.io, so it ships uncompressed and without a feature.

`geo_map_chart_engine.rs` measures a map into a retained scene from `GeoMapChart`, `MapFactory`, `CoreHeatLandSeries`, `LandAreaGeometry` and `MapViewportTransform`: the projector fitted into the draw margin (the title and legend room) and the lands clipped to its area, which is the plot of the scene; a collapsed draw margin hides the map; the visible layers by descending process index with the chart stroke and fill over the layer paints; the heat colour of each land of the default layer from the heat land series (a later series over an earlier one, a land of a departed series back to the chart fill, issue 962); the globe in the chart fill under an orthographic map, and each ring clipped at the horizon by the 15 bisections of the source and closed along the rim of the disc in steps of 3 degrees; all fills under one stroke of the lands (the source's `GeoMapStrokeZIndex`); the viewport of the source (`p * zoom + center * (1 - zoom) + pan` about the centre of the control, the stroke scaled by the zoom as the canvas matrix scales it), its zoom step (the speed clamped to [0.1, 0.95], the overshoot to 80 % of the minimum zoom, the pivot kept in place) and its bounce back; hover areas that hit the rings exactly, with the screen bounds of the land as the hover area of its point, the values of every series in their order and the anchor of the tooltip on the largest visible ring (the fix of the source for lands across the antimeridian); the lines of `SKDefaultGeoTooltip` (the land in title case, then `{series}: {value:N2}`); and markers. The lands are polygons and the stroke a path of the native renderer, which draws them on the GPU path of every backend.

`ferroui-controls-livecharts` adds `ChartKind::GeoMap` (`ChartKind` stays `non_exhaustive`), the generated `GeoMap` and `HeatLandSeries` (`Lands`, `ColorStops`, `MinValue`, `MaxValue`, and the two blues of the source as its `HeatMap`), the models `HeatLand` and `GeoMarker` with their collections `HeatLands` and `GeoMarkers`, `MapInteractionMode`, and the properties of `IGeoMapView` and `GeoMapChart` on the chart with the source defaults: `ActiveMap` (`None` is the world map), `MapProjection`, `MinLatitude`, `MaxLatitude`, `MinLongitude`, `MaxLongitude` (`None` is the default of the projection), `InteractionMode` (none by default), `MinZoomLevel`, `MaxZoomLevel`, `Stroke` (white), `Fill` (240, 240, 240), `CenterLongitude`, `CenterLatitude` and `Markers`. A map has no scene transition: the land colours of the source jump (`GeoMapAnimationTests`). It reports a click on a land at the release, unless the press moved more than 5 pixels; it zooms by wheel steps and pans by drag when `InteractionMode` allows, and a release settles the viewport with the bounce back of the source; `rotate_to` turns a globe the short way round, quadratic in and out; `project`, `unproject`, `find_land_at` and `map_land` give the projection and the lands. The ControlCatalog has the page GeoMap, and `snapshots.rs` restates the twelve images of the upstream `MapsTests` (86 scenarios in all); the custom map scenario reads the source sample's `mexico-states.geojson` (CC0, from Natural Earth), copied into `samples/LiveChartsDemo` with its attribution.

Differences from the source, named in the dispositions: a `GeoMarker` (a circle centred on its coordinate) stands for a `GeoVisualElement` around a visual; a hidden heat land series paints nothing and has no tooltip value, where the source measures every series; a land without a finite value is not painted; `HeatFunctions.InterpolateColor` takes the nearest stop where the source extrapolates before the first stop or divides by two stops at one offset; Mercator keeps the poles finite at 89.999999 degrees; a malformed GeoJSON file is an error that leaves the layer as it was, where the source throws halfway through it; maps are read from text, not from paths or streams; the native hover is not throttled; positions are `f64` where the source uses `float`.

`mapping.json` records as ported the 24 cases of `GeoProjectorsTesting`, the 11 of `HeatFunctionsTesting`, the 4 of `GeoJsonImportTests`, 13 of `GeoMapTests`, the 3 of `GeoVisualElementTests`, the 4 of `GeoMapEventsTests`, the 4 of `GeoMapReattachTests`, the 2 of `GeoMapAnimationTests`, `CollapsedDrawMarginAllChartsTests.GeoMap_HidesThenResets`, the 4 map cases of `ChartInteractiveApiTests` and `ChartTests.MapShouldHandleNullParams`; `GeoMap_EnumerateHeatLegendSources_ReturnsHeatLandSeries` and the eleven `MapsTests` declarations as partial; and 39 source files (the projectors, layers, lands, `HeatFunctions`, `HeatLand`, `MapViewportTransform`, `MapFactory` and the world map ported, `GeoMapChart`, `CoreHeatLandSeries`, `Maps`, `DrawnMap`, `IGeoMapView`, `GeoVisualElement`, the tooltip and the shared map view partial, the rest native replacements). The report counts 178 ported and 97 partial test declarations. `GeoMap_TooltipFormatter_IsAppliedToEachValue` stays pending with the tooltip formatters, `SharedUITests.MapChartTests` with the UI suites.

### L5: OHLC bars, error bars, date axes, heat series and the angular gauge

`ferroui-livecharts` closes the series and visuals that were not ported, each checked against the pinned source:

- OHLC bars: `SeriesKind::Ohlc` draws the I-bars of `OhlcGeometry` over the measure of `CoreFinancialSeries`: the spine from the high to the low, the open as a tick to the left and the close to the right, in the category width clamped to `MaxBarWidth` (25), stroked by the theme's up and down paints (a close above the open is up, as the source's `IsBullish`) at its thickness of 3, with the category-wide hover area of the financial series.
- Error bars: `ShowError` and `ErrorPaint` of the line, stacked area, bar, range bar and scatter series (`IErrorSeries`; a step, range line, box or financial series has none). The `ErrorVisual` of a point is a line across the value from its bottom to its top error and a line across the category from its left to its right error, through the point or the centre of the end of a bar beside the other bars of its category, on the axes a row series swaps, with no caps; the paint is the theme's (45, 45, 45) at the thickness 1 of a source paint. The errors widen the bounds whether they are drawn or not (`DataFactory`). The source draws the top error (`Yi`) of a bar or scatter point below it, against its own bounds, its line series and the documentation of `Error`; here it is above for every series.
- Labelers and date axes: `labelers.rs` ports `Labelers` (`SixRepresentativeDigits`, `FormatCurrency`, `Currency`, `BuildNamedLabeler`) and `AsDate` and `AsTimeSpan` (a negative value is the first date or zero); `Labeler` is the label function of an axis (the labels of an axis win over it, as `GetActualLabeler`); `Axis::date_time` and `Axis::time_span` are the `DateTimeAxis` and `TimeSpanAxis` of the source: the unit width and minimum step of the ticks of their unit and the labeler of the date or interval of a value. Dates are the .NET ticks of `DateTime` of `ferroui-base`, formatted with .NET format strings in the current culture.
- Heat series: the cells of `CoreHeatSeries` are inset by `PointPadding` (4 by default) in the hover area of the whole cell and coloured by `HeatFunctions` (the one of the map) between the weight bounds (`MinValue` and `MaxValue`, else the extremes of the weights) at the `ColorStops` of the heat map, the source's two blues by default; a heat map of fewer than two colours, or stops of another count, is an error where the source throws. A data label is centred on its cell with the primary value, the source's `Middle` position and default formatter.
- Angular gauge: `PieOptions::visual_elements` draws the `AngularTicksVisual` (the arc of the range from the truncated initial rotation over the maximum angle less 0.1, a tick and a label at each of ten nice separations of the range, four sub-ticks of half length in each interval, those before the minimum hidden) and the `NeedleVisual` (a triangle 45% of the smaller side long turned to its value, over a circle of its half width) of the source over the slices and under the data labels, in the theme's (30, 30, 30) and needle width of 20.
- Polar chart: each axis label is drawn over its `LabelsBackground` (the source's white; a transparent colour draws none), a rectangle of the text and `LabelsPadding` turned with it, and the labels of every axis over the separators of every axis; markers take `GeometryFill` and `GeometryStroke` (the series paint without them). The source measures the name of a polar axis but neither draws it nor reserves room for it, and a polar axis has no sub-separators, so neither is drawn here.

Everything is drawn as lines, paths, polygons, ellipses and text of the native renderer, which takes the GPU path of every backend.

`ferroui-controls-livecharts` adds the generated `OhlcSeries`, `DateTimeAxis` and `TimeSpanAxis` (an `Interval` sets the `UnitWidth` and `MinStep` of the axis and a `DateFormat` or `TimeFormat` its `Labeler`, as the source's `XamlDateTimeAxis` and `XamlTimeSpanAxis`; the defaults are a day and `d`, a millisecond and `fff` milliseconds), the `Labeler` (code only) and `LabelsBackground` of an axis, `ShowError`, `ErrorPaint`, `GeometryFill` and `GeometryStroke` of a series, the `ColorStops`, `PointPadding`, `MinValue` and `MaxValue` of `HeatSeries` with the source's heat map, and the `VisualElements` of a chart (`ChartVisualElements` of `ChartVisualElement`), with `AngularTicksVisual` (`Stroke`, `StrokeThickness`, `LabelsColor`, `OuterOffset`, `LabelsOuterOffset`, `TicksLength`, `LabelsSize`, `Labeler`) and `NeedleVisual` (`Value`, `Width`, `Translate`, `Fill`), which a pie chart draws and observes. The ControlCatalog pages CartesianChart and PieChart show OHLC bars on a date axis, error bars, a heat map and an angular gauge, and `snapshots.rs` restates `OhlcSeriesTests`, `ErrorSeriesTests`, `HeatSeriesTests`, `AngularGaugeTests`, `CandlesticksSeriesTests.DateTime`, `AxesTests.DateTimeScaled` and `DataLabelsTests.HeatWithLabels` (101 scenarios in all).

Differences from the source, named in the dispositions: the native default label of an axis stays the native number format, and `SetDefaultLabeler` has no counterpart; the formatter delegates of the XAML date axes are format strings (code sets any `Labeler`); `GaugeGenerator` has no native counterpart (the angular gauge scenarios build their sections); the heat legend (`SKHeatLegend`) and the data label positions of a heat series other than `Middle` are not ported; the error bars of a stacked bar are at the top of its stack, where the source takes the value of the point.

`mapping.json` records as ported the four cases of `LabelerTesting`, the three of `TimeSpanAxisTests`, `HeatSeriesTest.ShouldScale`, `RangeRowDateTimeAxisTests`, the three OHLC and two heat cases of `SeriesHitTests` and `_MemoryTests.HeatSeries_Releases`; the fifteen image scenarios as partial; and 23 source files: `OhlcGeometry`, `ErrorVisual`, `Labelers`, `DateTimeAxis`, `TimeSpanAxis`, `BaseAngularTicksVisual`, `BaseNeedleVisual` and `NeedleGeometry` ported, `CoreHeatSeries` partial, and `OhlcSeries`, `HeatSeries`, `IErrorSeries`, `IHeatSeries`, the heat layout and measure context, the error, date and time points, `BaseNeedleGeometry` and the SkiaSharp visual elements native replacements; `CorePolarAxis` and `CorePolarLineSeries` stay partial with less remaining. The report counts 193 ported and 112 partial test declarations. `HeatSeriesTest.ShouldPlaceToolTips` stays pending with the tooltip suites, `HeatSeriesTest.ShouldPlaceDataLabel` with the data label positions, and `OhlcSeriesAnimationTests` with the animation suites.
